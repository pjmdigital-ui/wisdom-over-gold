# January Outline — God First

Month theme: making God the center; seeking His Kingdom before self. Thesis verse: Matthew 6:33.

Arc: Week 1 names the problem (a life built around self). Week 2 is the turning point (recognizing it, surrendering). Week 3 is practical — what God-first looks like in each domain of a man's life. Week 4 is obstacles that pull him back. Days 29-31 close the month on renewal and commitment.

Do not reuse a scenario setup (same context/situation) across days. Vary domains: work, marriage, parenting, money, solitude, friendship, health, faith practice itself.
