# Day 31 — The Rest of the Year

Thirty days ago you sat in a truck, or a kitchen, or wherever your own version of that stillness happened, and felt the flat quiet of a goal that didn't deliver what it promised. Maybe it was a deal that closed and left you empty. Maybe it was just an ordinary Tuesday that finally cracked open the question you'd been outrunning for years: what are you actually building your life around? A month has passed since then. Not a transformed month, if you're honest — you snapped at your wife more than once, missed prayer some mornings, caught yourself back on the throne of your own life more times than you'd like to count. But something did shift. You know that now, because you can feel the difference between a man who's never asked the question and a man who's spent thirty days actually living inside it.

That's not nothing. That might be everything.

Matthew 6:33 opened this month, and it's worth hearing again now that it's had thirty days to work on you: "Seek first the kingdom of God and his righteousness, and all these things will be added to you." You've tested that verse for four weeks — in your marriage, at work, with your money, in the silence you forced yourself into more than once. You've seen where seeking Him first actually cost you something, and you're still here, which means you found out it was worth the cost. You've also seen how fast the old order reasserts itself the moment you stop paying attention — how quickly self climbs back onto a throne you thought you'd vacated for good. That's not a reason to think the month failed. It's the whole reason this has to be a practice and not a project, something you keep doing rather than something you finish.

Eleven months are still ahead. The pursuits still to come — of excellence, of relationship, of legacy — only mean anything built on top of this one. A man chasing excellence for his own glory is just building a more impressive version of the truck-in-the-driveway emptiness you started with. A man chasing it for God's glory has a foundation under him that doesn't shift when the win doesn't feel like enough. Everything this book covers from here depends on this month having actually taken root, not just been read.

**On St. Augustine:** He spent years chasing exactly what Day 1 described — status, pleasure, approval, certain each one would finally be enough — before he wrote the line that's followed him for sixteen centuries: "Our heart is restless until it rests in You." Restless is where he started. Rest is where he landed, and not because the chasing stopped being tempting, but because he finally let the chasing point him somewhere besides himself. That's the whole arc this month has been tracing in you. Not perfection — direction. Not a restless heart cured overnight, but a restless heart that has finally started, deliberately, again and again, to turn toward the One who can actually hold it.

You are not the god of your own life anymore, or at least you're further from that lie than you were thirty-one days ago. That's the only qualification this book requires going forward. Not that you've arrived. Just that you know now who you're actually seeking, and you're willing to keep seeking Him tomorrow, and the day after, for eleven more months and the rest of your life besides.

**Today:** Reread the one sentence you wrote on Day 1 — what you were chasing, and the words *this isn't big enough to be my god.* Underneath it, write one new sentence: what you're seeking now, thirty-one days later. Keep both. Let February find you still seeking.
