# June Outline — Friendship & Brotherhood

30 days. Closes Q2 (Protection). Accountability as protection — a man who tries to protect his family, character, and calling alone is more exposed, not less.

Belief being dismantled: *"I don't need anyone; asking for help is weakness."* Reframe: *"Iron sharpens iron; a man is only as strong as the brothers who can actually see him."*

Day 1 pays off the tease from May's closing entry (Proverbs 27:17, "Iron sharpens iron," was quoted once in May Day 31 and deliberately left undeveloped so June's Day 1 has full room to use it).

Arc: Week 1 names the self-sufficiency belief and its cost. Week 2 turns to real biblical and historical models of brotherhood: Peter and John, David and Jonathan, St. Paul's companions, St. Ambrose forming St. Augustine, and Sts. Basil the Great and Gregory Nazianzen. Week 3 is practical: actually calling someone, real accountability vs. surveillance, internal/external communication (the recurring thread's primary home this month), giving and receiving correction. Week 4 covers what derails brotherhood — surface-level friendship, competition, fear of being known, betrayal — and closes the month AND the entire Protection quarter, bridging into July (Work & Vocation, opening Q3/Provision).
