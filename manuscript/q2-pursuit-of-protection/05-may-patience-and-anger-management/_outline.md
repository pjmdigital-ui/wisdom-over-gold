# May Outline — Patience & Anger Management

31 days. Second month of Q2 (Protection). Direct continuation of April's theme: uncontrolled anger doesn't protect the people a man loves — it damages them. Character (April) holds up under pressure; this month is about the specific pressure of anger.

Belief being dismantled: *"My anger is justified; I need to react now to protect myself or my position."* Reframe: *"Patience is strength under control; anger surrendered instead of vented actually protects, where reacting only damages."*

Arc: Week 1 names the belief that immediate reaction equals strength, and that venting anger is just "being honest." Week 2 turns to real biblical wisdom on anger — Moses at the rock as the central cautionary example, patience defined as active self-command, not passivity. Week 3 is practical: anger across work, marriage, kids, traffic, the internet, and using the body to regulate response. Week 4 covers what derails this — simmering resentment, "righteous anger" as an excuse, damaged witness, repair — and closes bridging into June (Friendship & Brotherhood).

Note on Day 23 (Matthew 21:12-13, the temple cleansing): handle with real care. Jesus's zeal in the temple is not a blank check for a man's own anger — the point is the opposite of an excuse. Distinguish righteous zeal (aimed at real injustice, self-controlled, in service of others) from self-justified rage (aimed at personal offense, out of control, in service of ego). Don't let this day read as permission.
