# February Outline — Sacrifice & Struggle

28 days (standard February length). Month theme: suffering has meaning when united to Christ; sacrifice — voluntary cost — is how love gets proven, not a sign something's gone wrong. Belief being dismantled all month: *"Suffering means something's gone wrong; my job is to avoid discomfort."* Reframe: *"Suffering united to Christ has meaning; love is proven by what it costs."*

Arc: Week 1 names the modern instinct to avoid all discomfort and shows why it fails. Week 2 turns to the actual theology of redemptive suffering — union with Christ, not just endurance. Week 3 is practical: where deliberate sacrifice shows up across a man's actual life (work, marriage, time, money, body, ego, forgiveness). Week 4 covers what derails a man mid-suffering (self-pity, comparison, bitterness, quitting) and closes on renewal.

Every day follows the mindset-first structure locked in the style guide: scenario → name the specific false belief → scripture as the reframe (verify exact RSV-CE wording live, never from memory) → saint where it fits → new belief written/spoken → small action that follows from it → prayer (alternating address, fixed closing). Cut "it's not X, it's Y" constructions. Vary belief-reveal technique day to day. St. [Name] on first mention per entry, bare name after. Watch for repeated pet words across the whole month, not just per day.

Weave recurring threads where they genuinely fit this month (don't force): physical discipline/hard things (Day 19 is the natural home — training through discomfort), pride/humility (Day 20), keeping commitments (Day 24, not quitting on a promise). Point back to "Your List of Truths" on at least one discouragement-heavy day (Day 22 or 25 — writer's choice, whichever fits better once drafted).
