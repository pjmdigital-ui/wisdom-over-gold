# August Outline — Personal Finance & Stewardship

31 days. Continues Q3 (Provision). Where July asked what a man does with his hands and hours, August asks what he does with the money those hands and hours produce.

Belief being dismantled: *"This money is mine — I earned it, so what I do with it is nobody's business, including God's."* Reframe: *"Everything I have belongs to God; I'm a steward entrusted with it for a season, not an owner free to do whatever I want with it."*

Day 1 picks up directly from July Day 30's bridge (Matthew 25:21, "faithful over a little... set you over much") without reusing that verse — August opens by naming the ownership question underneath everything the money conversation touches.

Arc: Week 1 (Days 1-7) establishes stewardship vs. ownership as the foundational reframe — whose money it actually is, firstfruits giving, contentment, debt, the illusion of security in wealth. Week 2 (Days 8-14) turns to budgeting and planning as spiritual disciplines, not just practical chores — counting the cost, preparing ahead, delayed gratification, the two-masters tension, financial anxiety, prudence vs. presumption. Week 3 (Days 15-21) turns to generosity and family money culture — restitution, teaching children, honest money conversations with a spouse (this month's communication-thread instance), true wealth vs. status. Week 4 (Days 22-28) turns to legacy and the long view — long-term planning without idolizing comfort, providing for the next generation, financial hardship faced with faith, get-rich-quick temptation, the freedom of integrity, money as a tool for mission. Days 29-31 close the month: St. Francis of Assisi on radical detachment, a reflection-plus-bridge into September (Leading the Family/Household, closing Q3), and a bookend back to Day 1 (reprise Psalm 24:1).
