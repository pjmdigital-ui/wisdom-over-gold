# Saints Reference — By Month

Per the brief: saints are referenced where thematically appropriate, not forced into every entry. This is a working reference list matching saints to each month's theme; the daily entries pull from this list (roughly half of a month's days use a saint reference).

## Q1 — The Pursuit of Piety

**January — God First**
- St. Augustine of Hippo (restless heart, conversion from disordered self-love: "Our heart is restless until it rests in You")
- St. Ignatius of Loyola (ordering one's desires and priorities around God; the Principle and Foundation)
- St. Paul the Apostle (radical reordering of a life after encountering Christ)
- St. Francis of Assisi (renouncing wealth/status to put God first)
- St. Thomas Aquinas (subordinating brilliance and ambition to God)

**February — Sacrifice & Struggle**
- St. Maximilian Kolbe (literal self-sacrifice)
- St. Padre Pio (suffering united to Christ)
- St. Isaac Jogues (endurance through hardship)
- Job (biblical, not canonized — usable as a scriptural figure, not a "saint reference" line)
- St. John Vianney (relentless, exhausting daily labor as priest)

**March — Faith Under Doubt / Gratitude & Legacy**
- St. Thomas the Apostle (doubt met honestly)
- St. John Henry Newman (intellectual doubt resolved into deep faith)
- St. Monica (persistent faith and gratitude across decades)
- St. Thérèse of Lisieux (the "little way" — gratitude in small things)

## Q2 — The Pursuit of Protection

**April — Integrity & Character**
- St. Thomas More ("the King's good servant, but God's first")
- St. Joseph (quiet integrity, protecting without recognition)
- St. Maria Goretti's father figures / St. John the Baptist (speaking truth under pressure)

**May — Patience & Anger Management**
- St. Francis de Sales (patron of patience and gentleness in temperament)
- Moses (biblical figure on anger and self-command)
- St. John Bosco (patience with the difficult and the young)

**June — Friendship & Brotherhood**
- St. Peter and St. John (brotherhood among the apostles)
- St. Ambrose and St. Augustine (mentorship and accountability)
- Sts. Basil the Great and Gregory Nazianzen (deep, formative friendship)

## Q3 — The Pursuit of Provision

**July — Work & Vocation**
- St. Joseph the Worker (labor as vocation, not just wage)
- St. Isidore the Farmer (ordinary work sanctified)
- St. Benedict ("ora et labora" — work and prayer)

**August — Personal Finance & Stewardship**
- St. Matthew (former tax collector, converted relationship to money)
- St. Lawrence (steward of the Church's goods, generosity under pressure)
- St. Elizabeth Ann Seton (stewardship amid financial hardship)

**September — Leading the Family/Household**
- St. Joseph (head of the Holy Family)
- St. Louis IX of France (king and father, household leadership)
- St. Gianna Beretta Molla (sacrificial family leadership — usable from the husband's vantage point)

## Q4 — The Pursuit of Posterity

**October — Marriage & Love**
- Sts. Louis and Zélie Martin (married couple, canonized together, parents of St. Thérèse)
- St. John Paul II (theology of the body, married love)
- The Wedding at Cana (scriptural, not a saint)

**November — Fatherhood Fundamentals**
- St. Joseph (patron of fathers)
- St. Louis IX (father of eleven, raised for virtue and duty)
- St. Thomas More (father who formed his children's character deliberately)

**December — Hope & Presence**
- St. Joseph (waiting, presence, trust in darkness — fits Advent themes without being feast-day-bound)
- St. Nicholas (quiet generosity, presence to the overlooked)
- Simeon (biblical, patient hope fulfilled)

---
*Note: only January's saint references have been woven into drafted daily entries so far. This list is the working reference for the remaining eleven months when they're drafted.*
