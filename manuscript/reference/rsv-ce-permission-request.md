# RSV-CE Scripture Quotation Permission — Research & Draft Request

## What I found

**Who holds the rights:** The National Council of the Churches of Christ in the USA (NCC), through its affiliate Friendship Press, owns the RSV and RSV-CE copyright. Rights administration is currently outsourced to **Petradi International Rights Services** (took over from Riggins Rights Management in 2015).

- **Email:** NCCrights@petradirights.com
- **Phone:** (616) 375-0450
- **Mailing address:** 2500 East Beltline Ave SE, Suite G, Box 352, Grand Rapids, MI 49546

Note: this is the correct contact for the *original* RSV-CE (copyright 1965/1966, which matches the book's current placeholder notice). It is **not** the same as the RSV-2CE (the 2006 Ignatius Press revision) — that edition's permissions run through Ignatius Press instead. Since the manuscript's scripture notice already cites the 1965/1966 copyright, Petradi/NCC is the right desk.

**Gratis/fair-use threshold:** NCC's published policy allows quoting up to 500 verses, under 25% of the quoting work's total words, and less than one entire book of the Bible, without fee or written permission — if the text is unaltered and credited. This book likely clears the 25%-of-word-count and whole-book thresholds, but:
1. The book has an estimated 400–500+ total quotations, right at or over the 500-verse cap, and
2. NCC's policy explicitly excludes "Phone Applications or other New Media Platforms" from the gratis allowance — and it's genuinely unclear whether an EPUB counts as one.

Given that ambiguity, the safer path is requesting formal written permission rather than relying on self-assessed gratis eligibility.

**Fee/turnaround:** No public fee schedule — assessed case-by-case based on quotation volume and commercial vs. free distribution. No published turnaround time; the draft below asks for one directly.

**Sources:** friendshippress.org/pages/nrsvue-quick-faq · petradirights.com · rsvce.scribenet.com/specifications · rsv.scribenet.com · rigginsrights.com/about-us · Wikipedia (RSV-CE)
