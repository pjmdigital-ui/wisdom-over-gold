# Seek First: The Four Pursuits of the Modern Catholic Man
## Full Product Brief

---

## Core Thesis

The most important lesson of the author's life: to live to glorify and please God instead of himself — to make God the center of his world rather than being the god of his own life. Rooted in Matthew 6:33 — "Seek first the Kingdom of God." Every other pursuit in a man's life (protecting, providing, leading) only works rightly once this one is in place.

## Framework: The Four Pursuits

A man is never "finished" becoming any of these — he is always *in pursuit* of them, continually, for life:

1. **The Pursuit of God** — seeking His Kingdom first; ordering your life around Him rather than yourself. The foundation everything else stands on.
2. **The Pursuit of Protection** — becoming the guardian your family, marriage, and character need.
3. **The Pursuit of Provision** — stewarding work and money as calling and stewardship, not just hustle or duty.
4. **The Pursuit of Legacy** — patriarch; the man who leads his household and leaves something behind.

## Audience & Positioning

- **Who it's for:** Christian, specifically Catholic, fathers and family men trying to be the best father, husband, neighbor, friend, and businessman they can be.
- **Voice:** Standard devotional/reflective tone (not first-person "God speaking," like Jesus Calling). Written like a man who's actually in the trenches of fatherhood and business, not a monastery — earned wisdom, not lecture.
- **Tone comparison:** Inspired by the value found in existing men's devotionals and Jesus Calling, but built specifically for Catholic men navigating fatherhood, marriage, and business — a gap in the current market between overly academic Catholic resources and borrowed Protestant-tone devotionals.
- **Liturgical stance:** Independent of the liturgical/Church calendar and feast days, but includes references to saints where thematically appropriate (e.g., St. Joseph for fatherhood, St. Thomas More for integrity).

## Opening

The book opens with a **personal introduction** telling the author's own story — moving from cynicism and skepticism toward faith, and the specific lesson of learning to seek God's Kingdom first rather than living for himself. This intro carries the thesis; Month 1 then walks the reader through learning that same lesson for himself.

## Structure: 365 Days, 12 Months, 4 Quarters

Each quarter is built around one of the Four Pursuits, three months per quarter:

### Q1 — THE PURSUIT OF GOD
- **January — God First:** making God the center; seeking His Kingdom before self
- **February — Sacrifice & Struggle**
- **March — Faith Under Doubt / Gratitude & Legacy**

### Q2 — THE PURSUIT OF PROTECTION
- **April — Integrity & Character** (under pressure)
- **May — Patience & Anger Management**
- **June — Friendship & Brotherhood** (accountability as protection)

### Q3 — THE PURSUIT OF PROVISION
- **July — Work & Vocation** (purpose, calling)
- **August — Personal Finance & Stewardship**
- **September — Leading the Family/Household**

### Q4 — THE PURSUIT OF LEGACY
- **October — Marriage & Love**
- **November — Fatherhood Fundamentals**
- **December — Hope & Presence**

## Daily Entry Format (Hybrid)

Each day includes, in order:
1. **Real-world scenario** — a short, specific, non-generic moment (a fatherhood failure, a business decision, marriage friction) that opens the entry
2. **Scripture tie-in** — grounding the scenario in God's Word
3. **Short reflection** — connecting the scenario and scripture to the day's monthly theme
4. **Saint reference** (where it fits naturally) — tied to the month's theme, not forced into every entry
5. **One concrete action step** — a specific "do this today" line, not just a feeling to sit with

Target length: ~4-5 minute read per day.
