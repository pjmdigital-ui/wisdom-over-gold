# April Outline — Integrity & Character (Under Pressure)

30 days. Opens Q2 (Protection) — the man who cannot be trusted with the truth cannot actually protect anyone; character is the foundation everything else in this quarter stands on.

Belief being dismantled: *"What matters is how this looks, not what it costs someone unseen — small compromises don't really count if no one finds out."* Reframe: *"Character is who I am when no one's watching; integrity before God is the only integrity that counts."*

Arc: Week 1 names the belief that unseen compromises are free. Week 2 turns to St. Thomas More as the month's central figure — integrity that costs something real, examined closely. Week 3 is practical: integrity across business, marriage, money, speech, the body. Week 4 covers what derails integrity under real pressure (peer pressure, sunk-cost lying, fear of consequences) and closes the month bridging into May (Patience & Anger Management).

Every day follows the mindset-first structure in the style guide exactly: scenario → name the specific false belief → scripture as the reframe (verify exact RSV-CE wording live, never from memory) → saint where assigned → new belief written/spoken → small action → prayer (alternating address, fixed closing). No "it's not X, it's Y" constructions. Vary belief-reveal technique across your batch. St. [Name] full title on first mention per entry, bare name after — the "**On St. [Name]:**" saint-paragraph label always uses the full title regardless of what came before in the entry, but the paragraph body should NOT repeat the full title again right after the label (drop straight to the bare name in the body, matching the pattern used in every prior saint day in this book — do not write "**On St. Joseph:** St. Joseph was..."). Do not name any character "Paul" (reserved for St. Paul citations). Watch for repeated pet words across the whole month, not just per day. Avoid the "journey" cliché family entirely (banned as filler in the style guide).

This is the primary home for two recurring threads per the style guide:
- **Keeping promises and commitments** — Day 10 (Matthew 5:37, already verified) and Day 17.
- **Body language / presence** — Day 20. Ground it in the body as a truth-telling instrument (1 Corinthians 6:19-20), NOT in "power posing" research (Amy Cuddy has real replication problems, per the style guide) — lean on the theological point, not a secular productivity hack.
