# Scripture Notice

This book discusses plenty of Scripture, but you won't find a single Bible translation quoted directly anywhere in it. Every day's reflection is written in my own words, and at the end of each passage you'll see a bare reference — something like MATTHEW 6:33 — telling you exactly which passage inspired that day. That's on purpose. Take that reference, open your own Bible, whatever translation you trust, and read the passage in full context for yourself. This book isn't meant to replace Scripture. It's meant to send you back to it.
