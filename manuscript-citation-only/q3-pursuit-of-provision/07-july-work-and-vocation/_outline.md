# July Outline — Work & Vocation

31 days. Opens Q3 (Provision). Provision is bigger than a paycheck — it's the calling to steward what God has actually put in a man's hands, and work is the primary place that happens.

Belief being dismantled: *"Work is just what I do to survive until real life starts — it has nothing to do with my faith."* Reframe: *"My work is itself an act of worship and the concrete, daily way I love and provide for the people God has given me; how I do it matters as much as what I do."*

Day 1 pays off June Day 30's closing image (Proverbs 27:23, "know well the condition of your flocks... give attention to your herds") without reusing the verse itself — Foster's notebook line "I am ready to tend what God has actually given me to do" is the thread July's Day 1 picks up.

Arc: Week 1 (Days 1-7) establishes work as calling, not curse or compartment — dignity of work, work as worship, excellence as craftsmanship. Week 2 (Days 8-14) turns to diligence, integrity, and doing hard things on the job — direct callback to April's integrity thread and the "do hard things" thread. Week 3 (Days 15-21) turns to ambition, balance, and systems — workaholism, contentment, and this month's PRIMARY home for both the "systems over goals" thread (Day 17) and the "master one thing at a time" thread (Day 18). Week 4 (Days 22-28) turns outward to leadership, business relationships, and generosity through work. Days 29-31 close the month: St. Paul the tentmaker, an explicit bridge to August (money/stewardship), and a bookend back to Day 1's opening verse, closing July and setting up the rest of Q3.
