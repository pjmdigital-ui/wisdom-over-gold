# Day 25 — When God Says Wait

You applied for the promotion back in March. It's August now, and the job is still "under review." That's just a fancy way of saying nobody has decided yet, and nobody is telling you why. You've prayed about it in the car. You've prayed about it in the shower. You've prayed about it at 2 a.m., when your mind won't stop running the numbers on your mortgage. Nothing has changed. No sign. No open door. No answer that feels like an answer. Just the same silence you started with, five months in now.

Maybe it isn't a job for you. Maybe it's biopsy results that came back "we'll keep an eye on it." Maybe it's a marriage that's better but still not healed. Maybe it's a son who still won't call, or a business that hasn't turned around like you hoped. Somewhere around month four, a thought starts to form. You'd never say it out loud to another believer, but you let yourself think it: *If God really loved me, this would be over by now.* You don't say it to anyone. It just sits underneath everything else, changing how you hear the silence.

David knew this feeling. He wrote Psalm 27 while facing real danger, not during some calm, quiet season. And the trouble he's facing never gets solved by the end of the psalm. Instead, in Psalm 27:14, he tells his own heart to hold on for God, to stay strong, and not lose courage. Then he says it again, in that same verse: hold on. He says it twice, like a man talking himself down off a ledge. He doesn't say the wait will be short. He doesn't say he understands why it's taking so long. He just tells his own heart to hold on. That only makes sense if a long wait doesn't mean God loves you any less.

A father who loved his son less would end the wait faster, just to get it over with. God's timing depends on what He is building in you. Some things can only be built while you wait, in a place with no clock and no way out. Patience you didn't have before. Trust you hadn't earned yet. A habit of leaning on Him instead of your own plan for how this was supposed to go.

You don't have to feel strong to obey God's call to be strong. You just have to stay one more day. Let the waiting mean something different than what your worst 2 a.m. thoughts keep telling you.

**Today:** Name the thing you've been waiting on the longest. Then open your list of truths, the one you built in the front pages of this book. Read it slowly. Let it remind you what God has already proven about how He treats you. Do this before today's silence tries to tell you something else. Write today's date next to the thing you're waiting on. Add one sentence: *I don't know when, but I'm staying.*

**Prayer:** Lord God Almighty, I don't understand why this is taking so long. Some days it makes me doubt You love me at all. Remind me what You've already proven true. Give me the strength to keep waiting instead of running from it. I make this prayer in the almighty name of Jesus Christ, our Lord and Savior. Amen.
