# Day 8 — Seek First

He'd heard the verse a hundred times. He grew up hearing it, and he probably could have recited it half-asleep since he was twelve. Matthew 6:33 — seek first the kingdom of God, and everything else gets added. It was on a plaque in his grandmother's hallway, stitched into a throw pillow somewhere in his mother's house. It was the kind of verse that gets so familiar it stops landing anywhere at all. Church background noise. A nice sentiment for a needlepoint.

Then one ordinary Tuesday, sitting in his car in a parking lot before a meeting he didn't want to walk into, he heard it read out loud on the radio. It stopped him cold, the way a word you've read a thousand times can suddenly reorganize itself and mean something you'd never actually let it mean. *Seek first.* Not seek eventually, once the deal closes, once the kids are older, once the mortgage is paid off, once he's finally earned the right to slow down and get spiritual. First. Before. As the actual order of an actual life, not a value he'd get around to once the urgent stuff was handled.

That's the whole weight of this verse, and it's worth sitting with in full: seek first His kingdom and His righteousness, and everything else you need will be added on top of that. Jesus says this right after telling a crowd of working people not to worry about food, clothing, or tomorrow — the exact things a responsible man feels he ought to worry about most. Those needs still matter. Jesus is telling them where they belong in the order of things: after, not first. Downstream of trusting God, not upstream of it.

This is the verse this entire book stands on. It carries a diagnosis and a promise in the same breath. The diagnosis: most of us have built our lives around ourselves without ever calling it that. We seek our own comfort, security, and reputation first, and call whatever's left over for God devotion. The promise: reverse the order. Put God first, actually first, not eventually, and the things you've been white-knuckling to secure on your own get added. You didn't stop working for them. You stopped needing them to be your god.

That parking lot moment didn't fix his Tuesday. The meeting still happened. But something in him had reordered in the space of ninety seconds, and he couldn't unhear the verse the way he'd been not-hearing it for thirty years.

**On St. Paul:** He was riding to Damascus with letters authorizing him to arrest and imprison Christians. He was completely convinced his life was already in the right order, built around his own zeal, his own righteousness. A light on the road stopped him cold. Three days later he woke up a different man, reordered from the ground up around a Kingdom he'd been persecuting the day before. Nothing in his resume changed. Everything about what it was for did.

**Today:** Say Matthew 6:33 out loud, slowly, twice, before you do anything else today — before the phone, before the coffee. Then say this in your own words: "God, actually first today, not eventually." Notice one place you're tempted to put something else there instead.

**Prayer:** Father in Heaven, I've heard "seek first" my whole life without ever letting it reorder anything. Reorder me starting today — not once the deal closes or the mortgage is paid, but first. I make this prayer in the almighty name of Jesus Christ, our Lord and Savior. Amen.

MATTHEW 6:33
