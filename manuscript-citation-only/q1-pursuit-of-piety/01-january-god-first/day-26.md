# Day 26 — The Comparison Trap

You were only supposed to check the score. Ten seconds, phone in one hand, coffee in the other, standing at the kitchen counter before the kids were even up. But the app opened to your feed instead. And there he was, a guy you went to college with. Bigger house now. A boat, apparently. His family looked perfectly happy on some beach you couldn't even place. You knew a photo isn't a whole life. That didn't matter. Something in your chest tightened anyway.

By the time your own kids came downstairs, you snapped at them over nothing. Your wife asked what was wrong. You said "nothing," because *I feel like a failure over a boat I saw on Instagram* sounds as crazy out loud as it actually is. But under the irritation was a question you didn't say out loud either. What does he have that I don't? Is there some real peace, some put-together life, that he found and you're missing? On the drive to work, you kept comparing his one perfect morning to your whole ordinary week. You lost every time.

St. Paul answers this question in Galatians 6:4. He says every man should look closely at his own work and be honest about it. Take pride in what you've actually done, but measure it against yourself, not your neighbor. Paul knew you'd be tempted to measure yourself against the guy next door. He tells you plainly the only fair measurement is your own calling, your own work, the actual life God gave you to take care of.

Here's what the app never shows you. The guy with the boat has 2 a.m. worries too. Maybe a marriage under quiet strain. Maybe a body failing him somewhere. Maybe a father he still hasn't made peace with. You compared your whole life, the parts you actually live every day, to nine seconds he chose to post. Nine seconds is a highlight reel. It's the same kind of reel you'd post if you were building his page instead of your own.

A man who puts God first stops asking how he compares to the guy with the boat. He asks what God actually gave him to take care of. Then he asks if he's taking care of it well today.

**Today:** The next time you catch yourself measuring your life against someone else's feed, close the app. Say out loud: *I have exactly what God gave me to take care of.* Then name one specific thing right in front of you, your kids, your work, your marriage. Thank God for it.

**Prayer:** Father in Heaven, forgive me for grading my life against a stranger's highlight reel and feeling bitter about it. Help me see what You've actually put in front of me today. Help me thank You for it instead of resenting it. I make this prayer in the almighty name of Jesus Christ, our Lord and Savior. Amen.

GALATIANS 6:4
